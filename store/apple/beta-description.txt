Parle is a Safari extension plus a companion app for Mac, iPhone, and iPad. Open Parle beside a page to find public conversations on Hacker News, Reddit, Bluesky, Lemmy, and Lobsters, plus an archived copy and Wikipedia context. The companion shows only pages where you explicitly opened Parle, with direct links to the original, archive, and every discussion found. Recents stay only on that device for up to 100 pages or 30 days. There is no account, backend, analytics, or telemetry.
